Activated on June 30, 2026 at DAA score 474,165,565, the Toccata hard fork (Covenants++) sets the block header version to TOCCATA_BLOCK_VERSION = 2 (pre-Toccata: 1) and transaction version to TX_VERSION_TOCCATA = 1 (pre-Toccata: 0), while doubling the transient-mass limit to 1,000,000 grams.
